# Two languages: English and Spanish

Since 2026-11-04 Bot League (the pilot) and the portal speak English and Spanish. The user's
decisions (2026-11-04): the web texts are in English **and** Spanish; Bot League and the portal at
once; the language is chosen by the reader with a quiet offer when the browser asks for Spanish,
a cookie shared by the portal and the tools, and a switch in the foot. **Never by IP.** Code and
comments stay in English.

## How it works

- **Addresses.** English at the root, Spanish under `/es/` (`/board/` and `/es/board/`). Every page
  is built once per language as a static page: the page itself is `site/src/views/<Name>.astro`,
  which takes `lang`, and `src/pages/<path>.astro` and `src/pages/es/<path>.astro` are two lines each
  that render it. Each page carries `<html lang>`, its canonical, and `hreflang` en/es/x-default.
- **Words.** `site/src/i18n/<area>.ts`, one file per part of the site, with English and Spanish side
  by side: `export const board = words({ ...en }, { ...es })`. `words<T>(en: T, es: NoInfer<T>)`
  makes the type check (`make check`, `astro check`) fail if Spanish misses a key, has one too many,
  or turns a sentence with arguments into something else. Sentences with values in them are
  functions: `closeOf: (d: string) => \`close of ${d}\``. Written by hand in the session, reviewed by
  the user: no machine translation at run time and no paid API.
- **In a view** (built on the server): `const t = board[lang]` with the `lang` prop, and every link
  `link(path, lang)` (it adds `/es`). **In a page's script or a `lib/*.ts`** (in the browser):
  `const t = () => board[lang()]` or `board[lang()]` inside the function; `lang()` reads
  `<html lang>`. `link(path)` with no language is the page's own.
- **Numbers and dates** (`lib/format.ts`): `pct`, `signedPct`, `day`, `ordinal`, `timeAgo`, `num`,
  `plural` follow the page's language. Spanish: `+30,5 %`, `4 nov 2026`, `1.º`. Never `toFixed`
  plus `%` by hand in a text the reader sees: use `pct`/`num`.
- **The reader's choice.** The cookie `mh_lang` (`en`/`es`), on `.themarkethub.app` (one year,
  `SameSite=Lax`), set by the switch in the foot (`English · Español`) and by the offer.
  `Layout.astro` reads it in the head, before anything is drawn, and moves to the other language's
  page when it differs (`data-alt` on `<html>`). With no cookie, if the browser's first language is
  the other one, `i18n/choice.ts` shows a quiet bar ("¿Prefieres ver Bot League en español?",
  "Ver en español" / "No, gracias"); either answer sets the cookie. Search engines get no cookie
  and see each page in its own language.
- **What the service writes.** The page sends `X-MH-Lang: es|en` with every call (`apiHeaders()` in
  `lib/site.ts`). A header of our own, not `Accept-Language`: browsers and phones send that one by
  themselves, and the mobile app and the kit's `mh.py` must keep getting English. The server answers
  in Spanish only when `X-MH-Lang: es` (`lang_of(request)`). Data stays the same in both: only
  words change. A ticker, a company's name, a headline from a source and an opinion article stay as
  they are. Codes the page reads (a bot's `versions[].what`, `pending.what`) stay in English and the
  page words them (`said()` in `lib/doings.ts`).
- **Bot League's server** keeps its Spanish in `src/botleague/es.py`: `lang_of`, the factors' notes,
  and a table of every message the engine, the league, the chat and the API can say, applied at the
  edge so `engine.py` stays the portal's copy. A test fails if a new message has no Spanish row:
  a new `Refused(...)` needs its line there. `describe(bot, universe, lang)` is in the engine (and
  in the portal's `bots.py`). The chat tells Claude to answer in Spanish of Spain when the page is.
- **The portal's server** keeps its Spanish in `src/markethub/i18n.py`; it serves `/es/`,
  `/es/opinion/` and `/es/opinion/article/` from the Spanish builds, and the sitemap lists both
  languages with their hreflang. `/api/app/*` and token requests (the phone app) stay in English.
- **What stays in English for now**: text written by a model on the server (My Hub's sentences,
  the Watchlist's reads, the news the newswriter writes) and content from sources or authors (news,
  opinion articles, video titles). The user decided (2026-11-04) to leave what a model writes in English: Spanish would mean more model calls.
- **The cookie is on the privacy page** (portal `/privacy/`, both languages).
- **Money in Spanish** (portal `lib/format.ts`): `1,2 mil M$`, `45,6 M$`, `3,45 bill. $` (billón
  is 10^12 in Spanish), prices `185,40 $`. Spanish does not group four-digit numbers (`1057`).

## Style of the Spanish

Spanish of Spain, `tú`, short and plain, the same tone as the English: it describes, it never
advises. Typography: `«…»` for quotes, a space before `%` (`30,5 %`), decimal comma, `EE. UU.`,
months in lower case (`4 nov 2026`). Brand and product names stay: The Market Hub, Bot League,
My Hub, Claude, Claude Code, Codex, S&P 500, Yahoo Finance, TradingView.

## The rules in words (`describe`)

The engine (`engine.py`, copy of the portal's `bots.py`) and its copy in the page (`lib/words.ts`)
write a bot's rules in either language, with the same templates:

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| Looks at the {n} largest US companies[, leaving out A, B]. | Mira las {n} mayores empresas de EE. UU.[, sin contar A, B]. |
| Looks at {n} companies: A, B…. | Mira {n} empresas: A, B…. |
| Keeps those where {what} is {range}. | Se queda con aquellas en las que {what} es {range}. |
| Scores them on {what} (higher/lower is better, weight {w}); … | Las puntúa por {what} (mejor cuanto más alto/bajo, peso {w}); … |
| Holds the best {n}, in equal parts / the calmer ones with more / the best scored with more[, none above {x}%]. | Mantiene las {n} mejores, a partes iguales / con más peso para las más tranquilas / con más peso para las mejor puntuadas[, ninguna por encima del {x} %]. (n = 1: Mantiene la mejor, …) |
| Looks again at every close / the first close of each week / of each month. | Revisa la cartera en cada cierre / en el primer cierre de cada semana / de cada mes. |
| When, for the S&P 500, {what} is not {range}, holds {x}% in cash. | Cuando, para el S&P 500, {what} no es {range}, mantiene el {x} % en liquidez. |
| between {a} and {b} / at least {a} / at most {b} | entre {a} y {b} / al menos {a} / como mucho {b} |
| its return over {d} sessions[, leaving out the last {s}] | su rentabilidad en {d} sesiones[, sin contar las últimas {s}] |
| its {f}-session average against its {s}-session one | su media de {f} sesiones frente a la de {s} |
| its close against its {d}-session average | su cierre frente a su media de {d} sesiones |
| its volatility over {d} sessions | su volatilidad en {d} sesiones |
| its RSI over {d} sessions | su RSI en {d} sesiones |
| how far it is under its high of {d} sessions | su caída desde su máximo de {d} sesiones |
| its beta to the S&P 500 over {d} sessions | su beta al S&P 500 en {d} sesiones |
| figures: +25%, 0%, −20%, 0.5 | +25 %, 0 %, −20 %, 0,5 |
